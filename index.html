Significance to the Community

Proper waste segregation starts with quick, accessible education. SmartSorter AI bridges technology and sustainability:

1. Reducing Landfill Waste

Helps households and students instantly identify recyclable materials that can be repurposed into crafts instead of being thrown away.

2. Promoting Creative Upcycling

Demonstrates how everyday waste (like cardboard and plastic bottles) can be transformed into functional art, inspiring local eco-projects.

3. Accessible Environmental Education

Provides an interactive, hands-on tool for schools and barangays to teach proper sorting habits using accessible browser-based AI.

4. Preventing Recycling Contamination

Separating non-recyclable everyday items (like mixed-plastic pens) from pure recyclable crafts keeps community recycling streams clean.
